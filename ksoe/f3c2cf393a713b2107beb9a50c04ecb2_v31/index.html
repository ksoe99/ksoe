Gino D’Acampo Draws Over a Thousand Fans to Poplar Nurseries for Pizza Oven Launch

Newscasteruk
3 min read
·
Aug 25, 2025

--

It was standing room only at Poplar Nurseries in Marks Tey, Essex, as celebrity chef Gino D’Acampo brought his trademark charm — and a taste of Naples — to the garden centre for the launch of his new outdoor pizza oven range.

More than 1,000 fans flocked to see the popular TV personality, whose credits include I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here!, Gino’s Italian Escape, and Gordon, Gino and Fred’s Road Trip. Guests were treated to a cooking demonstration before lining up for a rare opportunity to chat with Gino, pose for photos, and receive expert tips on crafting the perfect pizza at home.

True to form, Gino brought energy and humour to the event. Poplar Nurseries Managing Director, Matthew Cowan, praised the chef’s generosity with his time and attention:

“Gino was brilliant — so friendly and engaging. He has a natural charisma and everyone, including staff and visitors, really enjoyed meeting him. He was only meant to stay for two hours but ended up staying nearly four, chatting to everyone.”

Throughout the afternoon, Gino signed books, asked guests about their favourite Italian dishes, and encouraged playful debates on the eternal question: pizza or pasta? His new line of outdoor pizza ovens, including the gas-fired Vesuvio and Modena models, was inspired by his passion for authentic Neapolitan-style pizza.

Poplar Nurseries , a family-run business since 1938, provided the perfect setting for the event. Well known for its extensive range of plants, garden essentials, and outdoor living products — from fire pits and patio heaters to Weber and Char-Griller barbecues — the venue underscored the communal, family-friendly atmosphere of the day.

The sheer excitement in the air was undeniable — fans of all ages, from local families to long-time foodies, waited patiently just for a few moments with Gino. Children lit up at his jokes, while adults spoke of how they’d followed his journey for years. For many, meeting him felt like reuniting with an old friend — a testament to his genuine connection with audiences far beyond the screen. His presence turned an ordinary afternoon into something truly memorable for the Essex community. As visitors left with smiles, signed books, and a few pizza-making secrets, one thing was clear: Gino D’Acampo had served up more than a product launch — he delivered an amazing experience
