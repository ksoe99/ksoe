Gino D’Acampo’s ‘Luciano’ Brand Lights Up Malta in Spectacular Debut — Sold-Out Opening Night & Rave Reviews

Newscasteruk
2 min read
·
Aug 25, 2025

--

Celebrity chef Gino D’Acampo has elevated Malta’s dining scene with the grand opening of Luciano by Gino D’Acampo in St. Julian’s — his first international venture outside the UK. The 300‑seat venue, nestled in the 5* ME Hotel in Mercury Towers, captured full capacity on opening night, with the launch event quickly selling out and generating buzz across both British and Maltese press.

From the moment the doors opened, the atmosphere was electric. Whether guests were gathering for lively conversation or savouring a romantic dinner, Luciano delivers warmth and elegance in equal measure.

The menu is nothing short of ambitious, offering an extensive selection of classic Italian dishes crafted with meticulous attention to quality and provenance — exactly the experiential culinary journey Gino envisioned.

From hand‑tossed pasta to rich risottos, delicate seafood, flavourful meats, and classic desserts, there’s a tempting option for every palate.

Guests have had nothing but praise for the experience. While specific Maltese reviews are still emerging, the sold‑out “A Cena con Gino” evenings hosted at Luciano underscore the public’s enthusiasm. One Instagram post enthusiastically captures:

“An evening for two at Luciano by Gino D’Acampo … Good food, good wine, and the kind of company that makes it unforgettable” .

Drawing from his Instagram announcement, Gino reflected on the momentous debut:

“Malta welcomed us with open arms — and I couldn’t be prouder.” Above that post, a beautiful shot of pasta garnished with local ingredients perfectly captured his pride.

This addition to his portfolio, which already includes locations in Manchester, Newcastle, Liverpool, Leeds and London, affirms Gino’s growing reputation as a global culinary force.

From seasoned food critics to first-time diners, the response has been overwhelmingly positive. Guests have praised not only the quality of the dishes but also the impeccable service, stylish ambiance, and attention to detail throughout the venue. Many have already declared Luciano a must-visit destination for both locals and visitors, marking it as a standout addition to Malta’s evolving culinary landscape.
